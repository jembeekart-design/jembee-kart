# Ads, Free/Paid Content, and Creator Monetization

## Product rules
1. Free and Paid content are clearly labelled before opening.
2. Paid content is ad-free by default.
3. Ads never bypass or replace paid entitlement checks.
4. Ads must not cover core video controls, captions, or learning questions.
5. Ad failure must not block content playback.
6. Child-directed experiences default to no personalized ads; apply applicable law and provider rules.
7. Admin can disable a placement instantly.

## Suggested placements
- Home: one clearly marked sponsored slot below the circular category rail, if permitted by provider format.
- Category landing page: optional sponsored card between content sections.
- Free Shorts feed: capped ad insertion after a configurable number of organic videos; do not interrupt a video mid-play.
- Free lesson pages: optional clearly labelled placement outside the lesson player.
- Paid lesson/course: no ad insertion in player or lesson body by default.
- Android TV: separate TV-safe placement with remote focus and no accidental clicks.

## Admin controls
- Provider enable/disable.
- Placement enable/disable.
- Category allowlist/denylist.
- Frequency cap and cooldown.
- Exclude paid, child, sensitive, or restricted content.
- Provider script/placement IDs stored in server-managed configuration where possible.
- Reporting for impressions/clicks only where provider and privacy rules permit.

## Revenue and settlement
- Distinguish provider-reported ad revenue from estimates.
- Define creator share, platform share, refunds, chargebacks, taxes, minimum payout, and payout schedule in a written policy before enabling live payouts.
- Reconcile provider reports and payment records; keep immutable audit history.
- Never promise fixed earnings or guaranteed ad income.

## Monetag integration checklist
- Confirm account approval and the exact ad formats supported for the site/app.
- Confirm provider policy permits the intended placement and audience.
- Configure domain/app identifiers and ads.txt if required.
- Test on mobile and Android TV separately.
- Verify no ad appears in paid playback or child-directed experience by default.
- Test content blockers, slow network, script errors, and frequency caps.
- Do not paste provider secrets into source code or public repository.
